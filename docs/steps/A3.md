# Step A3 — Pin names, read from the masters themselves

**Stage:** `python -m tools.puzzle pin-names` · **Gate:** `tools/checks/check_stepA3.py` → **46/46 PASS**
**Artifact:** `recon/derived/pin_names.json` · **Input:** `recon/derived/layers.json` (A1)
**Differentiator:** D2 — no PDK macro LEF

A1 found 8 `label` layers. A3 answers: **which of them carry pin names, and what are the names?**
Every name here is read out of `puzzle.gds`. No LEF, no library datasheet.

---

## 1. Method — which label layers are *pin*-label layers

A label annotates its layer, so a label pair is a pin-label layer for a given scope iff **its layer
number carries geometry in that scope**. Evaluated per scope, deliberately:

| scope | label layers | why |
|---|---|---|
| standard-cell masters | `64/5`, `64/59`, `67/5`, `68/5` | layers 64, 67, 68 carry geometry in the masters |
| the top cell | `70/5`, `71/5`, `72/5` | layers 70, 71, 72 only carry geometry in the top cell |
| excluded | `83/44` | layer 83 carries no geometry in **any** scope |

`83/44` is excluded by that structural test, not by a hardcoded list: its 73 labels *are* the cell
names (`a2111oi_2` …), and there is no layer 83 for them to annotate.

The per-scope distinction is load-bearing in both directions. The master power labels on `68/5` are
not top-level ports, and the port labels on `70/5` sit on a layer that never appears inside a master.

### A bug this step found in its own first attempt

The first version tested "do this label layer's own labels sit inside geometry on the same layer?"
That correctly excluded `83/44` but **wrongly excluded `64/59`**, the `VNB` body-tie label — because
the p-well region has no drawn geometry for it to sit in. The symptom was silent: `VNB` simply
vanished from the census while `VPB` stayed, giving 68 vs 69 masters. Nothing failed; the name was
absent. The refined test (does the *layer number* carry geometry **anywhere**, not just this
datatype on this pair) keeps `64/59` and still excludes `83/44`.

## 2. What came out

**69 masters · 34 distinct pin names · 803 pin labels** = `67/5` 521 + `68/5` 140 + `64/5` 71 +
`64/59` 71, each total reconciled against A1's independent per-layer `TEXT` count.

| pin name | masters | reading |
|---|---|---|
| `VPWR`, `VGND` | 69, 69 | every master; the two supplies |
| `VPB`, `VNB` | 68, 68 | body ties — every master **except** the tap cell (§4) |
| `X` | 36 | non-inverting output convention |
| `Y` | 26 | inverting output convention |
| `A1`, `A2`, `B1` | 31, 30, 27 | multi-input logic cells |
| `A`, `B` | 24, 24 | two-input logic cells |
| `C` | 14 | three-input cells |
| `D` | 9 | flip-flop data input, plus `D1` on one complex gate |
| `CLK`, `Q` | 3, 3 | the three flip-flop types |
| `RESET_B`, `SET_B`, `S` | 1, 1, 1 | one master each |

Other names, single-master: `A0`, `A4`, `A1_N`, `A2_N`, `B_N`, `B1_N`, `C_N`, `D_N`, `D1`, `HI`,
`LO`, `DIODE`. The trailing `_N` convention marks an active-low input, and `X`/`Y` never coexist in
a master.

## 3. The fifteen cross-checks — the real verification

Each check has **two independently derived sides**: one read from the labels *inside* the cells, the
other from the library's own name for the cell's function. They share no input, so agreement is
evidence the extraction is right rather than a restatement of itself.

| check | from labels | from names |
|---|---|---|
| `CLK` appears exactly on the flip-flops | `dfrtp_2`, `dfstp_2`, `dfxtp_2` | every `df*` type |
| `RESET_B` appears exactly on the `dfr*` cells | `dfrtp_2` | `dfr*` |
| `SET_B` appears exactly on the `dfs*` cells | `dfstp_2` | `dfs*` |
| the select pin `S` appears exactly on the `mux*` cells | `mux2_1` | `mux*` |
| a `Q` output appears exactly on the flip-flops | `dfrtp_2`, `dfstp_2`, `dfxtp_2` | every `df*` type |

plus structural invariants:

- no master has both an `X` and a `Y` pin;
- no master has both `Q` and an `X`/`Y` output;
- the only masters with **no output pin of any kind** are the physical cells — `tapvpwrvgnd` and
  `decap` — which is derived, not excused;
- every master has both `VPWR` and `VGND`;
- every master but the tap cell has `VPB`, and likewise `VNB`;
- the per-layer label totals match A1's (master scope), and the excluded layer's 73 labels are
  accounted for;
- the top cell exposes exactly Step 1's 13 port names, plus its own two power labels;
- the semantic anchors: `buf`→`X`, `inv`→`Y`, `and2`→`X`, `nand2`→`Y`, `or2`→`X`, `nor2`→`Y`,
  `xor2`→`X`, `xnor2`→`Y`, `mux2`→{`A0`,`A1`,`S`}, `dfrtp`→{`CLK`,`D`,`Q`,`RESET_B`},
  `dfstp`→`SET_B`, `conb`→{`HI`,`LO`}.

The `X`/`Y` split is itself consistent with the names: 36 masters expose `X` and 26 expose `Y`, and
`Y` is exactly the set of functions that invert (`inv`, `nand*`, `nor*`, `xnor2`, and every `o*`/`a*`
variant ending in `i`).

## 4. Details worth carrying forward

**The tie cell is real and has constant outputs.** `sky130_fd_sc_hd__conb_1` exposes `HI` and `LO`
alongside its supplies. The published work flagged tie cells as a trap — filtering them out leaves
flop reset pins undriven → unknown on reset. Here they are simply cells whose output pin happens to
be a constant, and A3 records them rather than treating them specially. `conb_1` is instantiated
6 times (from A2's placement counts), so it matters.

**The tap cell is the odd one out, consistently.** `tapvpwrvgnd_1` has only `VGND` and `VPWR` — no
`VPB`, no `VNB`, and it is the single master that A1 found lacking layer `236/0`. Three independent
signals agree that this cell is different.

**Clock buffers have no clock pin.** `clkbuf_4`, `clkbuf_8`, `clkbuf_16` expose `A` and `X` only.
Their names say they are meant for clock nets; their pins say they are ordinary buffers. Anything
inferring clock connectivity from names rather than pins would be wrong.

**Flip-flop reset/set polarity comes from the artifact.** `RESET_B` and `SET_B` are active-low by
their own names, and the three flop types separate cleanly: `dfr*` has `RESET_B`, `dfs*` has
`SET_B`, `dfx*` has neither. This is what lets A4/B6 build behavioural models without consulting
any library documentation — the polarity claim is grounded in the labels.

**The three flip-flop pin sets are exact** (asserted in the gate, since B5/B6 depend on them):

```
dfrtp_2 : CLK D Q RESET_B VGND VNB VPB VPWR
dfstp_2 : CLK D Q SET_B   VGND VNB VPB VPWR
dfxtp_2 : CLK D Q         VGND VNB VPB VPWR
```

## 5. What the gate asserts

45 checks: the stage exits 0 and reproduces the artifact byte-identically; the four extraction
totals; the per-layer reconciliation with A1; the derived label-layer scope and the exclusion
reason; all 15 cross-checks passing with both sides non-empty and equal on the load-bearing ones;
the census values for 11 names; the census being exactly the union of every master's pins; the exact
pin set of 8 named masters; every pin carrying a layer and a position and sitting only on master
pin-label layers; and the top cell's 13 ports plus its two power labels.
