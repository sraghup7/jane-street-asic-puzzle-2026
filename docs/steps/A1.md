# Step A1 — Layer roles, derived from the chip itself

**Stage:** `python -m tools.puzzle layers` · **Gate:** `tools/checks/check_stepA1.py` → **59/59 PASS**
**Artifact:** `recon/derived/layers.json` · **Differentiator:** D1 (the chip describes its own stack)

This step answers one question: *what is each of the 41 `(layer, datatype)` pairs in
`puzzle.gds` for?* The answer is derived entirely from structure inside the file. No tech file,
no layer map, no LEF. `tools/checks/check_hygiene.py` fails the build if any of those ever enters
the pipeline.

---

## 1. Method

Five measurements per pair, all from the file. Each role below names the rule that assigned it
(`R1`…`R9`), and the rule order is the whole method:

| Rule | Predicate | Role |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | carries `TEXT` and no geometry | `label` |
| R2 | is the file's pin datatype, on a layer that carries labels | `pin` |
| R3 | appears in a via master's layer set and is cut-shaped | `via_cut` |
| R4 | is bridged by a via master and used in the top cell | `routing` |
| R5 | is bridged by a via master and confined to masters | `local_wire` |
| R6 | cut-shaped but no via master uses it | `contact` |
| R7 | present ≤2×  per master and pinned to the cell footprint | `non_electrical` |
| R8 | other geometry belonging to the masters | `cell_geometry` |
| R9 | nothing above matched | `non_electrical` (subkind recorded) |

Two of the inputs are themselves **derived, not assumed**:

- **Site pitch = 0.46 µm.** Taken as the smallest positive difference between the distinct drawn
  cell widths (`tools/puzzle/layers.py::estimate_site_pitch`). Standard-cell widths are integer
  multiples of the placement site, so this is the pitch — no magic number.
- **Pin datatype = 16.** Taken as the datatype whose top-cell shapes stand in exact
  one-to-one correspondence with the top-cell labels *and* whose shape count equals the label
  count (`derive_pin_datatype`). Two weaker tests are not sufficient, and the gate pins down both
  failure modes: drawing layer `70/20` carries 45 shapes for 13 labels, and the power layers
  `71/16` / `72/16` each pass the one-shape-per-label test but carry 12 and 18 shapes for 2 labels.
  Only the count test separates the pin datatype from both.

## 2. Headline result — the connectivity chain

The 9 via masters decompose into **exactly two conductors and one cut each**, and the resulting
edges form a **single unbranched path** across six conductor layers:

```
67/20 —67/44— 68/20 —68/44— 69/20 —69/44— 70/20 —70/44— 71/20 —71/44— 72/20
 li1            met1          met2           met3          met4          met5
```

- `routing` (used as interconnect in the top cell): **68/20, 69/20, 70/20, 71/20, 72/20**
- `local_wire` (bridged, but confined to the masters): **67/20**
- `via_cut`: **67/44, 68/44, 69/44, 70/44, 71/44**

This is the operational form of differentiator **D1**: the chain is not inferred from layer-number
arithmetic or a technology file, it is read off the via masters' own geometry — and it closes. If
any layer had been misclassified, the chain would not be a simple path over six nodes. The gate
asserts the chain order, that it has exactly two endpoints and no branches, and that each adjacent
pair is bridged by exactly one cut layer.

The five non-chain layers of the standard stack are the cell-internal ones: `64/20` (well),
`65/20` (diffusion), `66/20` (poly), with `66/44` as the diffusion/poly contact and `67/16`,
`68/16` etc. as labelled pin geometry.

**Independent corroboration:** the via masters' placement counts sum to **8221**, which is exactly
the routed-via count Step 1 measured by a different method. Every one of the 9 via masters is
instantiated at least once.

## 4. What is *not* settled, stated plainly

Eight pairs are flagged `needs_review` (medium confidence). **None of them is connectivity-critical**
— the gate asserts that — so nothing downstream depends on them, but the uncertainty is real and
recorded rather than smoothed over.

| pair | role given | what is actually known | what is not |
|---|---|---|---|
| `64/20` | `cell_geometry` | one shape per master, ~1.6 µm tall, carries the `VPB` label; bridged into the stack via `64/16` and `65/44` | whether it is best called a well, a tap region, or a drawn well marker |
| `65/20`, `66/20` | `cell_geometry` | 215 / 358 shapes, shares area with `66/44` contacts; the sizes (0.27 × 2.51 stripes for `66/20`) are consistent with gate-like stripes | the exact device semantics — irrelevant here, because we reconstruct *connectivity*, not transistors |
| `65/44`, `66/15` | `cell_geometry` | two shapes each, in one master each; both are tiny and elongated | what they are for; they are not cut-shaped, so not contacts |
| `93/44`, `94/20` | `cell_geometry` | ≈1 shape per master, footprint-aligned, but with 3–4 distinct heights and no constant inset, so rules R7a/R7b both decline | whether these are process/select layers or device drawing |
| `95/20` | `cell_geometry` | 76 shapes in 67 masters, 30 distinct sizes, widths *not* on the site-pitch lattice | as above |
| `122/16` | `contact` | 0.17 µm squares, cut-shaped, one or two per master; its geometry is the `64/16` geometry **mirrored about the cell centre** (offset exactly 2.72 µm = the cell height); no via master uses it | most likely a mirrored pin/tap marker rather than a contact, on the evidence of that mirror relation |

`66/44` is also medium confidence but for a different reason: it is cut-shaped (2085 squares of
0.17 µm, the contact size) and clearly a contact, but no via master uses it, so its bridging
partners are not proven here. A2 will resolve it geometrically.

**Honest summary:** the roles that carry the design's connectivity are provable and proven. The
remainder are *annotated with their evidence* rather than assigned a confident label. Where a
semantic name would have been a guess, the role says `cell_geometry` and the evidence says why.

## 5. Reconciliation with Step 1

Step 1's dossier recorded **33** distinct `(layer, datatype)` pairs; this step finds **41**. The
difference is not a contradiction — it is two different measurements of the same file:

| Measurement | Pairs | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Step 1 `inventory.py` | 33 | iterates polygons and paths only |
| This step | 41 | also enumerates `TEXT`-bearing pairs |
| Difference | **8** | exactly `64/5`, `64/59`, `67/5`, `68/5`, `70/5`, `71/5`, `72/5`, `83/44` — all `label` |

33 + 8 = 41, and the gate asserts that identity against `inventory.json` rather than restating it.
Step 1's wording has been corrected in `docs/01_problem.md` to say "33 carry polygons or paths"
so the two documents cannot drift apart again.

Two smaller corrections that came out of this step:

- Step 1 described 13 top-cell port labels. The top cell actually carries **17** labels: 13 ports on
  `70/5` plus `VPWR`/`VGND` on `71/5` and `72/5`. The count is now pinned in the gate.
- `83/44` is a `label` layer, but it carries **cell names** (`a2111oi_2` …), not net names. It is a
  label layer by structure and must not be confused with the pin-label layers in A3.

## 6. What the gate asserts

59 checks, in six groups:

0. **Regenerability** — the stage is re-run with its output redirected to scratch (never over the
   committed artifact) and must reproduce `layers.json` byte for byte. Added during the
   post-Phase-A audit; see `docs/verification.md`.

1. **Completeness** — 41 pairs; every pair has a role from the known set, a named rule, and (if not
   high confidence) a note; the role table is an exact partition, with no pair in two roles, **and
   agrees with the role recorded on each pair itself** in both directions — the `roles` table is
   what a consumer reads, so drift between it and `pairs[].role` may not pass silently.
2. **The decisive cross-check** — every via master is exactly 2 conductors + 1 cut; the conductor
   adjacency graph is connected, has exactly two endpoints and no branches; the chain order is
   `67,68,69,70,71,72`; each adjacent pair is bridged by exactly one cut layer; and the five
   `routing` / `via_cut` / `pin` / `label` sets are pinned to exact expected values.
3. **Re-derivation of Step 1** — structure count, standard-cell master count, die bbox, the 68-of-69
   `236/0` marker, the two `INTERNAL_*` cells, the 13 port names, the 33 + 8 = 41 identity, and the
   8221 via-placement total all come back out of a *different* code path than Step 1 used.
4. **Honesty** — no connectivity-critical pair may be flagged for review, and every flagged pair
   must carry a recorded reason.
5. **The `summary` block is derived data too** — its pair count, role histogram, review list and
   per-pair review flag must all equal the derivation from `pairs`. Also added by the audit: both
   of these blocks were previously written but never checked against what they summarise.
