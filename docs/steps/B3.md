warm-up could not answer at scale: *is the extraction complete*, and *can the supply nets be
identified by what they are rather than by their size*.

## 2. Supply is identified by name, not by size

The two largest nets carry **only** supply pin names: `VGND`+`VNB` on one, `VPWR` on the
other. No functional pin name appears on either. That is the plan's stronger method — the
number of supply pins read off those nets (1608 `VPWR`, 1608 `VGND`, 932 `VNB`) matches the
1618 placements, and the two biggest nets are the two biggest by *polygon count* as well, so
the identification does not depend on which size metric is chosen.

The one name missing from the supply nets is `VPB` — and it is missing for the reason A5
predicted (§3), which is a cross-check rather than an inconsistency.

## 3. A5's prediction, confirmed at scale

A5 ended with two known gap classes: the `VPB` well ties, which have no routing geometry, and
the antenna diode's supplies. B3 probes **every** supply pin of every one of the 1618
placements — 5120 probes — and finds **972** that the engine cannot place in a net:

| | count |
|---|---|
| `VPB` well ties | 942 |
| `diode_2` supplies (`VGND`, `VNB`, `VPWR`) | 30 |
| **outside those two classes** | **0** |

Zero unexplained. This is what `docs/verification.md` §7 asked for when it recorded that A5's
two gap classes "are expected and must not be reported as errors by later integrity checks" —
B3 now *confirms* them instead of rediscovering them, and would fail loudly if a third class
appeared.

## 4. Two dead ends worth recording

Both were reached by trying the cheaper-looking path first, and both are the kind of thing
that would silently produce a wrong answer if not checked.

### 4.1 Per-net polygon counts do not reconcile with a layer's polygon count

The obvious completeness check is `Σ_nets |polygons of that net| == |polygons of the layer|`.
It cannot work here: `shapes_of_net` returns **per-net merged** regions while a layer's region
is not merged the same way, so the two quantities are not comparable. Measured on 67/20:
per-net sum 13 029 vs layer 16 869 — a gap that looks like 3 840 orphans and is actually
merge bookkeeping.

A set-difference attempt (`layer region − union of net regions`) was worse: accumulated across
the hierarchy it flattened the regions and reported **2 315 phantom orphans** on 67/20 and 234
on 68/20. That is the failure mode to avoid — an orphan check that invents orphans.

So the check is the plan's literal one: probe **every individual shape**. It costs 1.5 s.

### 4.2 A recursive shape's geometry is in its own cell's coordinates

The first run of that probe reported **31 844 orphans**, every one of them at coordinates
under 1 µm. `top.begin_shapes_rec(layer)` walks the hierarchy but hands back each shape in
its *own* cell's coordinate system; without applying the iterator's transform
(`poly.transformed(it.trans())`) every subcell shape is probed at local coordinates and lands
on nothing. This is the fourth coordinate-system bug in the project (two micron/DBU, one
bounding-box-vs-polygon, this one) — and the second one that produced a plausible-looking
wrong number rather than an exception.

### 4.3 Interior points, again

Probing a shape needs a point inside it, and B2 already established that a bounding-box centre
is not one. `connect.interior_point` tries the centre, then the shoelace centroid, then points
stepped in from each vertex toward the centroid. Across 40 360 shapes it failed **zero** times,
which is the evidence that the completeness check is exhaustive rather than partial.

## 5. An artifact may not contain a clock

The first version stored `runtime_s` in `nets.json` and the gate's byte-identical regeneration
check failed immediately. A committed artifact that embeds a wall-clock duration can never
reproduce byte-for-byte, which is a property every stage in this project relies on. Runtimes
are now printed and measured **by the gate**, never stored; the gate also asserts that no
timing field is present. Cost: 2 s for extract + probe at this scale, so the reporting
requirement is satisfied by stdout.

supply names; and the size metric agreeing.

## 7. Carried forward

1. **B4 gets a namespace, not an identity — and it has to fix that.** `cluster_id` is the net
   key, but it is unique only *within one circuit*. The 9839 subcircuits are exactly one level
   deep (1618 cell placements + 8221 via placements), so a probe inside a cell returns that
   cell's *local* net: 70 unrelated nets shared `cluster_id == 2`. Flattening before extraction
   (`connect.build_engine`) leaves one circuit and 2626 distinct ids. B4's per-instance pin
   mapping must resolve instance identity through the B1 table, never through the engine's
   subcircuit objects. B4 found this and implemented the fix — see `docs/verification.md` §8.
2. **972 supply pins will have no net.** B4's verify clause says "the count of unassigned pins
   must be 0 for functional pins" — that stays true, but supply pins are a documented
   exception with a closed list of two causes, and B4 must reproduce that list rather than
   treat it as a failure.
3. **The `VPB` net is not in the extraction** — the n-well ties have no routeable geometry, so
   any later check that expects a well net must expect its absence.

## 8. Amendment, 2026-09-15: the diode supplies are not a gap class

An external review (R2) found that A4 missed 135 GDSII paths the masters draw (128 met1 supply
rails, 7 li1 segments), which made the antenna diode's `VGND`/`VNB`/`VPWR` look unrouted — see
`docs/steps/A4.md` §9. §3 above ("A5's prediction, confirmed at scale") reports that artifact as a
genuine second gap class; it was one class (the `VPB` well ties, 942) all along.

With the fix, all 1618 diode-supply placements the paths were missing now reach their net: the
`VGND`+`VNB` net grows from 1608/932 to **1618 `VGND` + 942 `VNB`**, and the `VPWR` net from 1608 to
**1618**. Supply probes unassigned falls from 972 to **942**, all `VPB`. §1's two result blocks and
§2–3's narrative are the pre-fix numbers, kept as produced; the gate (36/36) now asserts the
corrected ones.
